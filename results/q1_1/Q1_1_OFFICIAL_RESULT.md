## Official model result

For three known, error-free transmitter positions `(q_a,q_b,q_c)`, let the receiving UAV's own raw local signature be `(y_ab,y_ac,y_bc)`. For each nonboundary angle, construct both constant-angle circle-center branches

\[
\rho=\frac{\|A-B\|}{2\sin\theta},\qquad d_\perp=\rho|\cos\theta|.
\]

For each choice of two angle constraints among `ab`, `ac`, and `bc`, enumerate all intersections of the corresponding circle branches. Reject transmitter/receiver coincidences, duplicates, and every point that fails the three original unsigned `atan2` angle evaluations. The unused angle is the branch/order holdout for that two-constraint construction.

The output is the complete finite candidate set, not a forced single point. A point may be certified only inside its receiver-numbered local slot domain when that domain contains one candidate and the selected two-angle Jacobian has rank two. This is a local, nondegenerate statement; it is not a global uniqueness claim.

The independent checker is a deterministic multi-start damped Gauss-Newton solve with finite-difference Jacobian; it contains no circle construction and validates the third angle after solving a two-angle pair. Its agreement is an implementation cross-check, not external validation.

## Explicit failure semantics

- `CANDIDATE`: one or more finite points survive all raw-angle checks.
- `CERTIFIED`: requires a receiver-local slot-domain uniqueness condition plus full local rank; the deterministic Gate does not assign a production slot-domain radius.
- `REJECTED`: exact `0/pi` boundary input, transmitter/receiver coincidence, or insufficient branch evidence.

The following are intentionally not claimed: global uniqueness, arbitrary-initial-position recoverability, robustness to unmodelled physical noise, or an externally validated location estimate.
